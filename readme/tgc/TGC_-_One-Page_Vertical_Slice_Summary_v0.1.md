# TGC — One-Page Vertical Slice Summary v0.1

## Purpose

The first playable vertical slice proves the foundation of **That Game: Catalyst**.

It does not attempt to prove the full feature set. It proves the core experience:

> **TGC feels like a socially alive multiplayer world worth building on.**

The slice should show that players can enter a shared space, communicate naturally, cooperate on a practical task, explore a nearby mystery, change the local world state, and leave wanting to continue.

---

## Recommended Scenario

## The Relay Beneath the Ridge

A small Nova Terra settlement is experiencing intermittent power and communication failures.

VERITAS attributes the issue to environmental instability. Harmony asks the players to restore local systems and investigate inconsistent readings near a nearby ridge.

Players gather in the settlement, communicate using proximity and directional voice, repair local infrastructure, explore a nearby alien anomaly, discover a contradiction in VERITAS records, and return to a visibly improved settlement.

---

## Player Flow

1. Players arrive in the settlement.
2. Harmony introduces movement, communication, and basic interaction.
3. Players locate and understand each other using proximity audio.
4. VERITAS identifies a damaged relay or power node.
5. Players gather basic resources nearby.
6. Players repair or build the relay component together.
7. The relay partially restores settlement function.
8. The restored system reveals anomalous readings from the ridge.
9. Players travel to the point of interest.
10. They discover a partially buried alien structure.
11. A scan produces data VERITAS cannot fully reconcile.
12. A light hazard, puzzle, or hostile system response occurs.
13. Players recover an artefact fragment or corrupted record.
14. They return to the settlement.
15. The settlement gains a visible improvement.
16. VERITAS revises the mission record or Harmony expresses concern.
17. A new unresolved question is introduced.

## What The Slice Proves

### Social Presence

Players feel physically present with each other in the same world.

The slice proves readable player position, body presence, proximity awareness, directional audio, distance falloff, and comfortable local conversation.

### Communication Context

Players understand who is speaking, where they are, and whether communication is local, grouped, or mediated.

The slice proves local proximity voice, small group communication, speaker readability, channel clarity, and basic comfort controls.

### Shared World Interaction

Players can work together on a practical task that changes the environment.

The slice proves simple resource collection, repair or construction, shared contribution, practical outcome, and visible world-state change.

### Exploration and Mystery

Players discover something useful and unresolved beyond the settlement.

The slice proves short-range exploration, environmental readability, point-of-interest discovery, artefact or clue interaction, and uncertainty around VERITAS or the official record.

### Narrative Through Action

Story is introduced through player activity, not heavy exposition.

The slice proves VERITAS or Harmony as useful interfaces, alien traces as playable mystery, and settlement change as narrative consequence.

## Core Required Systems

1. Multiplayer session support
2. Third-person player movement
3. Directional proximity voice
4. Basic group communication
5. Player identity and speaker readability
6. Basic social comfort controls
7. Settlement environment
8. Simple resource collection
9. Simple building, repair, or placement
10. Basic inventory interaction
11. Simple crafting or construction requirement
12. One guided mission or activity
13. One exploration point of interest
14. VERITAS or Harmony interaction
15. One mystery clue or contradiction
16. Visible world-state change

## Explicitly Out Of Scope

The first slice does not prove everything.

Out of scope:

- full economy
- full faction simulation
- full governance
- full crafting professions
- advanced automation
- large-scale settlement management
- multi-planet travel
- stock market systems
- succession and inheritance
- complex wildlife ecology
- large-scale terraforming
- full PvP systems
- full campaign structure
- deep alien metaphysics
- complete character arcs
- large numbers of worlds
- endgame loops

These systems matter, but they come later.

---

## Success Criteria

The vertical slice succeeds when:

- players naturally talk in-world
- players understand where voices come from
- players gather comfortably in a shared space
- players cooperate on a practical task
- players see their actions change the environment
- players discover something mysterious
- players understand VERITAS or Harmony as useful interfaces
- players feel the world has social and narrative depth
- players want to continue exploring or building after the objective ends

The strongest success signal:

> **Players stay in the world after the objective is complete because being there with others feels good.**
