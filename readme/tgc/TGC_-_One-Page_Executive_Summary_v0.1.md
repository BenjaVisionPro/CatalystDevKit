# TGC — One-Page Executive Summary v0.1

## Project Positioning

**That Game: Catalyst** is a multiplayer social world built around presence, communication, shared agency, and player-shaped civilisation.

The project is expressed through a third-person sci-fi civilisation sandbox, but its foundation is human socialisation. Clean directional multiplayer sound, proximity-aware communication, environmental audio, group coordination, comfortable social spaces, and seamless collaboration are core infrastructure.

TGC is not defined by feature quantity. It is defined by how its systems combine into a coherent social experience:

> **A comfortable, expressive, socially alive frontier where players communicate naturally, shape the world together, and create shared history.**

---

## Player Experience

Players enter a living frontier where they explore alien worlds, build settlements, gather resources, craft tools, trade, govern, join factions, complete missions, uncover ruins, and investigate hidden forces shaping human expansion.

These activities are not separate genre features. They are social engines.

- Building creates places where people gather.
- Crafting creates specialisation, trade, and reputation.
- Exploration creates shared discovery and risk.
- Factions create identity, pressure, and conflict.
- Economy creates trust and negotiation.
- Governance creates consequence.
- Narrative gives meaning to what players uncover and build.

The product is not just what players can do. The product is how it feels to do those things with other people.

---

## Core Differentiator

Most multiplayer games treat communication as an external utility.

TGC treats communication as part of the world.

The social architecture is built around:

- proximity voice
- clean directional sound
- distance falloff
- group communication
- private and public communication contexts
- settlement, faction, and mission communication
- environmental acoustics
- comfort and moderation controls
- future support for translation, AI listening, altered speech, broadcasts, and diegetic communication devices

Players should feel physically and socially present with each other before any mission, building system, faction system, or combat encounter asks them to cooperate.

---

## World and Narrative

TGC begins after humanity’s Horizon mission has reached Novus Astra under altered and uncertain conditions.

Human settlers have built new societies, uncovered evidence of earlier alien civilisations, and fractured into competing factions with different visions for the future.

Narrative functions as gameplay infrastructure:

- **VERITAS** guides players, preserves records, interprets the world, and cannot be fully trusted.
- **Harmony** provides an embodied, emotionally accessible AI interface.
- **The Veiled Directive** creates long-form mystery and uncertainty.
- **Alien ruins and artefacts** drive exploration, progression, conflict, and discovery.
- **Factions** turn ideology, resources, technology, ecology, freedom, tradition, and control into playable social pressure.

Story gives meaning to shared action. It explains why the world matters, why players gather, why factions disagree, and why building civilisation is more than resource management.

## First Playable Proof

The recommended vertical slice is **The Relay Beneath the Ridge**.

A small Nova Terra settlement experiences power and communication failures. Players gather, communicate, repair local infrastructure, explore a nearby alien anomaly, discover a contradiction in VERITAS records, and return to a visibly improved settlement.

The slice proves:

- in-world social presence
- clean directional voice
- comfortable local communication
- basic group coordination
- movement and embodied presence
- settlement space
- simple resource collection
- simple building or repair
- shared objective flow
- VERITAS or Harmony guidance
- environmental discovery
- one mystery clue
- visible world-state change

The slice is deliberately small. It proves that being in the world with other people feels good, useful, and worth continuing.

---

## Stakeholder Takeaway

TGC is a socially grounded multiplayer world where clean communication, shared presence, player-shaped environments, and long-form discovery combine into a civilisation sandbox people want to inhabit together.

The project succeeds when players stay in the world not only because there are objectives to complete, but because being there with others feels natural, useful, expressive, and meaningful.
